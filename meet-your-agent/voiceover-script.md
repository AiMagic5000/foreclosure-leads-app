# Meet Your Agents — Owner Podcast Voiceover Script

**Use:** Voiceover for the "Meet Your Agents" landing page. Two company owners, Corey and Allie, talking podcast-style. Establishes that the agents reaching out to claimants are real and vetted, then explains the surplus and how to move forward.
**Format:** Two AI avatars (Corey = male, Allie = female). Can render as one combined podcast video or two solo segments cut together.
**Voice:** MiniMax 2.5 (`speech-02-hd`). Allie = `English_Insightful_Speaker` / Calm_Woman. Corey = calm, trustworthy male preset.
**Length:** ~2 minutes.
**USFR rules applied:** says "state" not "county"; owners speak as "we / our company," first person only for self-introduction; straight quotes; no disclaimers.

---

## SCRIPT

**COREY:** Hi, I'm Corey, and along with Allie here, I help run Foreclosure Recovery Inc. If you are watching this, it is because one of our agents reached out to you, and we wanted you to see the real people behind that call or that email.

**ALLIE:** That is the first thing we want you to know. The agent contacting you works for us. They are real, they are vetted by our company, and we stand behind every one of them. So if you got a message from us and you were not sure whether it was legitimate, here we are. It is real.

**COREY:** And the reason any of us reached out is simple. When your property sold at the foreclosure auction, it sold for more than you owed. After the bank, the city, the auctioneer, and everyone else with a claim was paid in full, there was money left over. That extra money is called a surplus, or an overage, and by law it belongs to you. Not the bank. Not the state. You.

**ALLIE:** Here is what most people never find out in time. Every state sets a deadline. If that window closes and no one has properly claimed the funds, the state gets to keep the money. They are in no rush to track you down. A lot of the time the only notice goes to the old foreclosed address, the one place they already knew you had left. So the letter goes nowhere, the clock runs out, and the money quietly becomes theirs.

**COREY:** That is exactly why we went looking for you. We found you, we confirmed the funds are real, and we want them back in your hands before that deadline passes.

**ALLIE:** And we want to be straight with you about how we work. We do not get paid until you get paid. We cannot move a single dollar without your permission and your involvement, so you stay in control the whole way through. Our fee is a contingency. It is written out plainly in the agreement we sent you, and it only comes out once your money comes through.

**COREY:** The process is built to be quick and simple. Take a few minutes to review the contingency agreement attached to the email we sent. It is only a few pages. If anything is unclear, call the agent who reached out. Their number is right there in the email.

**ALLIE:** When you are ready, sign the signature page, take a picture, and send it back by email or text. That is it. We start on your case right away. And before you decide, look us up. Check our reviews. You will see that we operate legally and we treat people right, because the only way this works is if you get paid.

**COREY:** Our support team is here seven days a week, so you are never left wondering what comes next.

**ALLIE:** You worked hard for years paying into that home. That money is yours.

**COREY:** And we would be honored to help you bring it home.
