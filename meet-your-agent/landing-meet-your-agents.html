Why we reached out

When your property sold at auction, it sold for more than you owed. After the bank, the city, the auctioneer, and everyone else with a claim was paid in full, there was money left over. That surplus, also called an overage, belongs to you. Not the bank. Not the state. You.

Every state sets a deadline. If that window closes and no one claims the funds, the state keeps the money, and they are in no rush to find you. The only notice often goes to the old foreclosed address you already left. That is why we tracked you down, confirmed the funds are real, and want them back in your hands before the deadline passes.

We do not get paid until you get paid. We cannot move a single dollar without your permission, so you stay in control the whole way through. Our fee is a contingency, written plainly in the agreement, and it only comes out once your money comes through.
