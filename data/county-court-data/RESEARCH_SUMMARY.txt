RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
====================

SUCCESSFUL APPROACHES
---------------------
1. Official State Directories
   - NY: NYS Association of County Clerks (NYSAC) - comprehensive tables
   - OH: Ohio Clerk of Courts Association (OCCA) - full directory
   - Provided verified, complete contact information

2. WebFetch Tool
   - Successfully extracted tabular data from official HTML directories
   - Preserved formatting and accuracy

3. WebSearch Pattern
   - "[County] County [State] clerk of court phone fax"
   - Targeted individual county searches

CHALLENGES ENCOUNTERED
---------------------
1. North Carolina PDF Documents
   - NCACC directory PDFs (2024, 2025, 2026) contain PDF encoding artifacts
   - Email addresses readable but phone/fax numbers in binary format
   - Fetches from NCACC PDFs returned unreadable content

2. County-Specific Websites
   - NC counties host clerk info on individual government sites
   - No centralized searchable database like NY and OH
   - Information scattered across county portals

3. E-Filing URLs
   - Most counties use statewide systems
   - County-specific filing portals rare for all three states
