================================================================================
  STATEWIDE E-FILING SYSTEMS (100% DOCUMENTED)
================================================================================

GEORGIA (159 counties)
├─ System: Odyssey eFileGA (Tyler Technologies)
├─ URL: https://efilega.tylertech.cloud/OfsEfsp/ui/landing
├─ Coverage: All 159 Superior Courts
└─ Directory: https://gaclerks.org/Clerks/FindMyClerk.aspx

HAWAII (5 counties)
├─ System: JEFS (Judiciary Electronic Filing and Service System)
├─ URL: https://jimspss1.courts.state.hi.us/JIMSExternal/login.iface
├─ Counties: Hawaii, Honolulu, Kalawao, Kauai, Maui
└─ Directory: https://www.courts.state.hi.us/

IDAHO (44 counties)
├─ System: iCourt / File & Serve (Tyler Technologies)
├─ URL: https://idaho.tylertech.cloud/ofsweb/
├─ Coverage: All 44 counties
└─ Directory: https://voteidaho.gov/county-clerk/ (with CSV export)

ILLINOIS (102 counties)
├─ System: eFileIL (Tyler Technologies)
├─ URL: https://efile.illinoiscourts.gov/
├─ Coverage: All 102 counties via Electronic Filing Service Providers
└─ Directory: https://www.illinoiscourts.gov/courts/circuit-court/circuit-court-clerks/

INDIANA (92 counties)
├─ System: Odyssey → INfile (Spring 2026)
├─ URL: https://efile.incourts.gov/ (current)
├─ Coverage: All 92 counties
└─ Directory: https://www.in.gov/courts/directory/ (with PDF export)

IOWA (99 counties)
├─ System: EDMS (Electronic Document Management System)
├─ URL: https://www.iowacourts.gov/efile
├─ Coverage: All 99 counties (fully statewide since 2015)
└─ Directory: https://das.iowa.gov/county-clerk-court-contacts

KANSAS (105 counties)
├─ System: Kansas eCourt (statewide case management + e-filing)
├─ URL: https://filer.kscourts.org/
├─ Coverage: All 105 counties (fully live as of Nov 5, 2024)
└─ Directory: http://www.kcceoa.org/county-clerks (by district)

KENTUCKY (120 counties)
├─ System: File & Serve (Kentucky Court of Justice)
├─ URL: https://www.kycourts.gov/AOC/Information-and-Technology/Pages/File_Serve(eFiling).aspx
├─ Coverage: All 120 counties (available since Oct 2015)
└─ Directory: https://kentuckycountyclerks.com/ (searchable)
