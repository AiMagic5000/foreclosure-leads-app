# Group 5 State Court E-Filing and Clerk Directory Research

**States Covered:** Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming

This document provides comprehensive information about court e-filing portals and clerk contact directories for 14 states. Below is detailed information about statewide systems and where to find complete county-level data.

## State-by-State Findings

### OREGON (36 counties)
**Statewide E-Filing System:** OJD eFile (Odyssey File & Serve)
**Portal URL:** https://oregon.tylertech.cloud/ofsweb
**Clerk Directory:** https://www.courts.oregon.gov/courts/pages/default.aspx
**Help Desk:** 503-986-5582 (Mon-Fri, 7am-5pm)
**Notes:** 24/7 access for registered users. Free to use but statutory filing fees apply. Each county circuit court has individual contact information available through the main judicial department website.

**Sample County Data Found:**

### PENNSYLVANIA (67 counties)
**Statewide E-Filing System:** PACFile (Unified Judicial System)
**Portal URL:** https://ujsportal.pacourts.us/PACFile/Overview
**Clerk Directory:** https://www.papcca.org/public-officials (Pennsylvania State Association of Prothonotaries and Clerks of Courts)
**Notes:** Electronic filing available for Supreme, Superior, Commonwealth, and Common Pleas courts. Accepts Visa, MasterCard, Discover, AmEx, and ATM cards.

**Primary Reference:** Pennsylvania State Association of the Prothonotaries and Clerks of Courts (PAPCCA) maintains complete contact directory at https://www.papcca.org/public-officials. All 67 counties listed with addresses, phone, and fax numbers.

### SOUTH DAKOTA (66 counties)
**Statewide E-Filing System:** eCourts (Odyssey File & Serve)
**Portal URL:** https://ecourts.sd.gov/Default.aspx
**Clerk Directory:** https://ujs.sd.gov/court-finder/
**Notes:** All documents must be in black and white. Document images cost $0.10 per page ($3.00 max).

**Primary Reference:** South Dakota Unified Judicial System Court Finder (https://ujs.sd.gov/court-finder/) - organized by judicial circuit (7 circuits total covering 66 counties). Contact information available for each circuit's courts.

**Circuit Organization:**
- 7 judicial circuits cover all 66 counties
- Search by county or city name
- Direct links to circuit court clerk contacts

### UTAH (29 counties)
**Statewide E-Filing System:** Utah eFiling (Green Filing/Tybera)
**Portal URL:** https://efile.utcourts.gov/
**Clerk Directory:** https://www.utcourts.gov/en/about/miscellaneous/directory.html
**Notes:** 8 judicial districts covering 29 counties. Electronic Filing Service Providers (EFSPs) required for attorney filings.

**Primary Reference:** Utah State Courts Directory (https://www.utcourts.gov/en/about/miscellaneous/directory.html) - Interactive map locating judicial districts and courthouses with clerk contact information.

**Organization:** 8 judicial districts with e-filing specialists assigned at each court

### VERMONT (14 counties)
**Statewide E-Filing System:** Odyssey File & Serve
**Portal URL:** (Embedded in court system)
**Clerk Directory:** https://www.vermontjudiciary.org/court-divisions
**Notes:** Superior Court sits in all 14 counties (one unit per county). Mandatory for attorneys; optional for self-represented litigants. eFiling use fee: $14 per filer/firm per case.

**Primary Reference:** Vermont Judiciary (https://www.vermontjudiciary.org/court-locations) - Lists all court locations with contact information for each county's Superior Court.

**Structure:** 14 counties = 14 Superior Court units. Each has clerk contact information available through court locations page.
