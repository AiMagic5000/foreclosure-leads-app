## Lessons Learned

1. **Fax numbers are disappearing** - Most modern government agencies only publish phone numbers
2. **County-specific e-filing is inconsistent** - Some counties have dedicated portals, others use statewide only
3. **No centralized government database** - Each state manages data differently
4. **Georgia is most challenging** - No single searchable directory, requires county-by-county lookup
5. **South Carolina is most complete** - Official judicial branch maintains comprehensive clerk roster

---

## Recommendations for Future Research Batches

1. **Prioritize phone + address collection first** - These are 90%+ available
2. **Plan for county-by-county fax lookups** - Budget 2-5 minutes per county for fax research
3. **Target county government websites directly** - Better success than statewide aggregators
4. **Consider bulk purchasing from data vendors** - May be more cost-effective than manual research
5. **Verify e-filing URLs directly with clerks** - Statewide portals often have county endpoints not publicly listed
