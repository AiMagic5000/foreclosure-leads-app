Step 2 — Turn on your voicemail helper

This tool leaves a friendly voicemail on people’s phones for you. Their phone never even rings.

1. In the left menu, find the Communications section and click Ringless Drips:

2. On that page you will see a green box called “Set up SlyBroadcast”. It shows you exactly what to do:

3. In short: make a free account at slybroadcast.com, put $10 or $20 on it (that lasts for months), then type your SlyBroadcast email and password into your My Account page.

IMPORTANT:

For your Sly Broadcast account to work be sure to enable API Access.

When you log in to your Sly Broadcast account and go to:

To turn on API access, log into your SlyBroadcast account and click the round account icon in the top-right corner, then choose Integrations. Find the green suitcase tile labeled Slybroadcast API Access and click Enable API so it reads “API Enabled”:

Next, open your business-email webmail and go to the Templates folder (just under your Inbox). A ready-to-send message asking SlyBroadcast to activate the API is waiting there. Open it, use the three-dot menu at the top right, choose Edit as new, add your SlyBroadcast account email where noted, and press Send. They usually enable it within about 48 hours.

Then log out of your account at: https://usforeclosureleads.com/ and then log back in and your voice-drop communications should work.
